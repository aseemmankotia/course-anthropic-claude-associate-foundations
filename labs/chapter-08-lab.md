# Chapter 8 Lab: Troubleshooting and Optimization

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Take a real underperforming prompt that produced a vague or generic output, diagnose the root cause (missing context, ambiguous ask, wrong format), and rewrite it to produce a measurably better result

## Key takeaway

Most output problems trace back to missing context or ambiguous instructions, not a hard capability limit — diagnose before you rewrite.
