# Chapter 3 Lab: Workflow Integration and Solution Design

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Map a real recurring workflow (e.g., producing a monthly status report) into individual steps, then mark each step as Claude-suitable, human-only, or hybrid, and justify each decision in one sentence

## Key takeaway

Good workflow integration means assigning Claude to specific steps it handles well, not handing it an entire process.
