# Chapter 6 Lab: Product and Model Selection

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Given five short workplace tasks of varying complexity and length, select the most appropriate Claude surface (chat, Project, research mode, Artifact) and model tier (Haiku, Sonnet, Opus) for each, with a one-line justification

## Key takeaway

Match the Claude surface and model tier to the task's complexity and persistence needs — more powerful isn't always better.
