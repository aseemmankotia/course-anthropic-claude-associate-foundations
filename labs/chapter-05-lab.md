# Chapter 5 Lab: Prompting and Task Execution

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Write a first prompt to summarize a messy, unstructured meeting transcript, then iterate it three times based on specific gaps in the output (missing action items, wrong tone, excessive length) until the summary meets a defined standard

## Key takeaway

Effective prompting means decomposing complex tasks and iterating with specific feedback, not writing one perfect prompt on the first try.
