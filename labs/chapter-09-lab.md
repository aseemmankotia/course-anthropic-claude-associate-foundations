# Chapter 9 Lab: Full Exam Simulation and Scoring Strategy

**Estimated time:** 120 minutes

## Exercise

Complete a full 60-question practice exam simulation covering all seven domains under realistic 120-minute timed conditions, then review a domain-by-domain score breakdown to identify the weakest areas for final review

Treat the 720/1000 threshold as a scaled bar, not a percentage, and practice full-length timed pacing before exam day.
