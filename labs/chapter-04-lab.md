# Chapter 4 Lab: Governance, Risk, and Responsible Use

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Complete a responsible-use decision exercise: review five short workplace scenarios involving customer data, HR records, and public content, and decide for each whether using Claude is appropriate, appropriate with redaction, or not appropriate

## Key takeaway

Responsible use starts with classifying data sensitivity and matching the use case to organizational policy before a prompt is ever sent.
