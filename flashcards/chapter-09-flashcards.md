## Chapter 9: Full Exam Simulation and Scoring Strategy — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | CCAO-F exam length and time limit? | 60 questions, 120 minutes — roughly 2 minutes per question average. Exam tip: budget less than 2 min for easy single-response items to bank time for multiple-response items. |
| 2 | CCAO-F passing score? | 720 out of 1000 on a scaled score. Exam tip: this is NOT 72% of 60 raw questions — scaled scores weight items by difficulty and domain. |
| 3 | Why doesn't 45/60 correct (75%) guarantee a passing scaled score? | Scaled scoring (0-1000, pass at 720) adjusts for item difficulty and domain performance, so raw percent correct does not map 1:1 to the final score. |
| 4 | Heaviest-weighted domain on CCAO-F? | Output Evaluation at 21%. Exam tip: when time-constrained, prioritize reviewing flagged Output Evaluation questions first for maximum score impact. |
| 5 | CCAO-F certification validity period? | 12 months. Exam tip: renewal completed on time is free and non-proctored — missing the window forfeits that benefit. |
| 6 | What happens if you let the 12-month renewal window lapse? | You lose eligibility for the free, non-proctored renewal and must retake a full proctored exam. Exam tip: renew before expiration, not after. |
| 7 | Checkpoint pacing rule for a 60-question, 120-minute exam? | At question 30 you should have used ~60 minutes (2 min/question average). Ahead of this = buffer for review; behind = need to speed up. |
| 8 | Flag-and-return strategy — core rule? | Mark uncertain questions, continue forward without dwelling, then return to flagged items with remaining time, reviewing highest domain-weight questions first. |
| 9 | Second-guessing rule under time pressure? | Do not change a confirmed answer without a concrete new reason. Changing answers based on anxiety alone more often converts correct answers to incorrect ones. |
| 10 | Multiple-response scoring assumption to avoid? | No partial credit — all correct options must be selected for the item to score correct. Read the full stem and every option carefully before answering. |
| 11 | Time allocation for multiple-response vs single-response items? | Multiple-response items take longer than the 2-minute average; budget extra time for them and recover time on quick single-response items. |
| 12 | How should final review time be distributed across flagged questions from different domains? | Proportionally to domain exam weight — spend the most review time on flagged items from the heaviest-weighted domain (Output Evaluation, 21%) first. |
